## The four steps

1. **Choose a concept** and write its name at the top of a blank page.
2. **Explain it to a beginner** in the simplest language possible.
3. **Identify gaps** — places where you stall or hide behind jargon.
4. **Review and simplify**: go back to the material, fill the gaps, and explain again.

## How I use it

I used to study by highlighting and re-reading. Everything felt familiar, yet I could not reproduce an explanation once the book was closed.
With the Feynman technique, every note I write must answer three questions:

- What problem does it solve?
- Why does it work — not just what the steps are?
- Can I explain it with an everyday analogy?

> The test of understanding is not "I read it" but "I can explain it".

## Connection to this site

This website is my Feynman notebook: every entry is written so that a stranger could understand it.
